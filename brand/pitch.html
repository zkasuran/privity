Why now

The regulator’s proposal describes the event we built.

For uncertificated securities, prompt posting and turnaround are effectively the same event.
Morgan Lewis, summarising the SEC’s proposed Rules 17ad-2 and 17ad-10, September 2026. Comments close 3 Nov 2026.

A transfer and a register update should be one event once securities stop being pieces of paper. Privity makes them literally one transaction.

3
Why Canton

Two properties that no public chain offers together.

A public chain

Gives you atomic settlement. Also publishes every investor's position to every other investor. For a regulated fund that ends the conversation.

Off-ledger

Gives you confidentiality. Also keeps cash and register as two separate events, which is the problem itself.

Canton's sub-transaction privacy means one transaction commits across parties while each participant validates only the views its own parties are stakeholders on.

We did not take this on trust

Our first design had the buyer read the seller's holding to verify the trade. The ledger rejected it, because the buyer is not a stakeholder. Canton enforced the confidentiality property against our own code.

4
The design

Calibrated disclosure, not maximum secrecy.

The obvious repair was to add the buyer as an observer on the seller's holding. That would have disclosed the seller's entire position. Both versions compile. Both settle correctly. Only a test written to falsify the claim tells them apart.

Hide everything

The buyer cannot verify what it is paying for. Unusable.

Disclose the holding

The buyer learns the seller's whole book. Pointless.

Disclose one parcel

The seller splits exactly the quantity being sold. The buyer verifies that and learns nothing else. The transfer then clears the disclosure.

The engineering is finding the floor between those two failures. For a secondary trade that floor is exactly one parcel.

What is built

Three packages. The disclosure layer stands alone on purpose.

PackageContentsWhy separate
privity-disclosureMandates, windows, audit recordsZero fund logic, so any Canton app can depend on it
privityFund, register, cash, NAV, settlementThe application
privity-testsDaml Script suiteKeeps test tooling out of production DARs

Both sides sign a mandate

If only the auditor signed, anyone could assert supervision over anyone. If only the subject signed, a firm could manufacture evidence of an audit that never happened.

Upgrade-safe, the hard way

Moving mandate validity to ledger time meant deleting a choice field. Canton refused the upload. The field is retained and ignored, so 1.1.0 vets as a valid upgrade of 1.0.0.

8
What is not done

Stated here rather than discovered by a judge.

the real gap

Zero practitioner interviews

Problem evidence is documentary and regulator-sourced, which moves problem validation and not market validation. The instrument and a named target list are built. A falsification test is committed in writing: if three ICP interviews say the timing gap is already handled, the wedge is wrong and the product moves.

scope, deliberately

Single synchronizer, LocalNet only

A Daml transaction can only consume contracts on one synchronizer, so atomicity is demonstrated in that topology, which is what the Global Synchronizer provides. The cross-synchronizer case is documented, needs no model change, and carries no settlement risk because no value moves until the atomic step.

Nothing deployed beyond LocalNet. No production integration with an admin system, which is the largest practical adoption risk.

9
Who buys, and how

A small, named, regulated buyer set.

The person

  • Pain owner: fund accounting or transfer agency lead at a mid-tier administrator, 20 to 60 funds
  • Economic buyer: head of operations
  • Influencer: the auditor. Not the buyer.

The model

  • Per fund per year, plus a platform fee
  • Not per seat: the value is process containment, not user productivity
  • Land one administrator, expand across its funds. One sale, thirty deployments

On the neighbour in this track

Alluvren solves the front-end redemption decisioning layer. Privity solves the back-end bilateral settlement and disclosure layer. They may coexist in the same fund workflow.

Operator guidance

What a participant operator considers acceptable for holding a share register in production.
