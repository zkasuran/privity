## Outreach message + interview script (ready to send/use)

**LinkedIn:**
> Hi {name}, I'm researching how private fund subscriptions get from cash received to units on the register. Not selling anything. Could I ask 15 minutes of questions about how your last one went? Happy to share what I learn.

**Ecosystem ask:**
> I'm building Privity (atomic fund DvP on Canton) for HackCanton. Could you intro one fund accounting or TA lead at an administrator, for 15 minutes of questions, not a pitch?

**15-minute script** (product only at minute 10):
1. (0–3) Walk me through your last subscription, from signed docs to units in the register. Who touches it?
2. (3–6) When did the cash land, and when were the units recorded? What is the worst gap in the last year? *Evidence = a number, or "did not know".*
3. (6–8) What happened with the last wire that had no reference?
4. (8–10) Who may see the register? What would be unacceptable on a shared ledger? *Evidence = a refusal they raise without prompting, captured verbatim.*
5. (10) **Falsifier:** is the cash/register gap already handled well enough by your bank and reconciliation workflow? Would you change process for it?
6. (11–13) Describe Privity in one breath. Which part matters? What did I get wrong?
7. (13–15) What would a sandbox trial need? Who signed off your last process change? Who else should I ask? May I quote you anonymously?
